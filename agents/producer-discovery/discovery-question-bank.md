# Discovery Question Bank

Starter library of discovery questions the agent draws from when populating the "Extended Discovery Question Bank" section of the output. Meant to be **replaced** with the actual SDP question library from OneNote — this is a placeholder set that is directionally correct for a benefits consulting engagement.

The agent should **filter and reword** these for each prospect. A question the producer already has the answer to (from the Specs Sheet) does not belong in the discovery output.

Each question includes a `Why` line. Producers can strip it when transferring into their call notes.

---

## Business context

- **What's changing in your business right now that's putting pressure on your benefits program?**
  - Why: Surfaces the trigger event driving the search. If they say "nothing," you're in a slow deal.
- **How has your headcount changed over the past 24 months, and what's your projection for the next 12?**
  - Why: Cross-check against 5500 growth; also anchors ancillary and stop-loss conversations.
- **Where are you hiring, and what does your geographic footprint look like today vs. two years ago?**
  - Why: Multi-state complexity drives compliance work and carrier-selection considerations.
- **Are there any pending or recent transactions — M&A, divestitures, PE recap — we should know about?**
  - Why: Deal timing and plan-transition risk are major discovery items.

## Current benefits program

- **Walk me through your current plan lineup and how you got to it.**
  - Why: Open-ended; reveals decision history and pain points without leading.
- **Which parts of the current program are you most proud of? Least proud of?**
  - Why: Producers often hear only the complaints; the "proud of" answer tells you what's off-limits to change.
- **How did you land on your current funding arrangement?**
  - Why: If the answer is "we've always done it this way," there's an opening.
- **When was the last time you meaningfully changed vendors, and how did that go?**
  - Why: Predicts appetite for change.
- **What's your renewal timing? What does the renewal process look like internally?**
  - Why: Anchors the whole sales timeline.

## Financial & budget

- **How is benefits budgeted — as a fixed line, a % of payroll, or a shared cost with employees?**
  - Why: Framing determines who cares about savings and who cares about employee experience.
- **What's your target trend, and what have you actually been running?**
  - Why: The gap between target and actual is where a broker earns their seat.
- **How much have employee contributions moved in the past three years?**
  - Why: Signals affordability pressure and executive commitment.
- **Are there any budget or headcount changes coming in the next fiscal year we should factor in?**
  - Why: Contextualizes proposal timing.

## Employee experience

- **What does your workforce actually think of the current benefits program? How do you know?**
  - Why: Distinguishes clients who measure from clients who guess.
- **Where do employees push back — cost, network, service, communication, tech?**
  - Why: Different pushback categories point to different solutions.
- **How do you communicate benefits today, and what's working / not working?**
  - Why: Communication and enrollment tech are often the easiest early wins.
- **Are there any populations (union, hourly, remote, international) with distinct needs?**
  - Why: Complicates carrier selection and plan design.

## Compliance & risk

- **How do you handle ERISA compliance today — internally, through your current broker, or a specialist?**
  - Why: Reveals gaps you can fill.
- **Have you had any ERISA claims, DOL audits, or plan-related litigation in the past three years?**
  - Why: Sensitive but important. Ask carefully.
- **Are you comfortable with your fiduciary governance — committee cadence, meeting minutes, investment policy statements where applicable?**
  - Why: Under-invested area for most mid-market employers.
- **How are you handling the recent [insert current-year regulatory item — RxDC, gag clause attestation, MHPAEA NQTL analysis, etc.]?**
  - Why: Time-sensitive and often unaddressed.

## Vendor & broker relationships

- **How long have you been with your current broker, and how did you originally select them?**
  - Why: Long tenure without a re-bid is an opening; recent selection is a defensive posture.
- **What does your current broker do exceptionally well? What do you wish they did more of?**
  - Why: Frames the gap you're proposing to fill without disparaging the incumbent.
- **How often do you formally evaluate your broker, and what's that process?**
  - Why: If they don't, propose it. If they do, learn the criteria.
- **What's your current service model — dedicated team, service center, mixed?**
  - Why: Sets the bar for what your service proposal must beat.

## Decision process

- **Who else needs to be involved in an evaluation like this?**
  - Why: Buying committee mapping. Ask early.
- **What does your decision timeline look like, and what are the milestones?**
  - Why: Convert vague timing into a schedule you can plan around.
- **What has to be true for you to make a change?**
  - Why: The single best question in this whole bank. Do not skip.
- **What has stopped you from making a change in the past?**
  - Why: Predicts objections you'll face this time.
- **If we were to move forward, how would we know 12 months in that it was the right call?**
  - Why: Anchors success metrics that inform your proposal.

## Vertical-specific addenda

The agent should include only the addendum for the prospect's vertical. Add or replace verticals as your firm's appetite dictates.

### Manufacturing
- What percentage of your workforce is hourly vs. salaried, and how does that affect enrollment and communication?
- How do you handle workers' comp interplay with your health plan?
- Any collective bargaining coming up?

### Professional services / firms
- How do you think about benefits as a talent retention tool vs. a cost line?
- Do partners/owners have a different plan than staff?
- Are you tracking productivity or absence signals?

### Healthcare
- Do you have a health plan for your own employees separate from what you deliver to patients?
- How do you handle the higher-utilization workforce risk?
- What's your position on domestic partner benefits and reproductive care in your states of operation?

### Technology / SaaS
- Where does benefits sit on your total-rewards priority list vs. equity and cash?
- Any remote-first or distributed-workforce implications for carrier selection?
- How do you handle international team members?

### Non-profit / education
- Are you subject to any funder-imposed benefits requirements?
- Church plan or governmental plan status — does it apply here?
- Volunteer / part-time populations with distinct needs?

### Private equity portfolio company
- Does the sponsor mandate any platform benefits programs?
- Is there a value-creation plan tied to benefits costs?
- Sponsor exit timeline — how does that affect a multi-year benefits strategy?

### Construction / high-hazard
- Any multi-employer plan participation?
- How do the workers' comp mod and health plan interact?
- Prevailing wage / Davis-Bacon considerations?

## Rules for how the agent selects questions

1. **Ceiling of ~15 questions in the Extended bank.** More is not better.
2. **Never repeat a Specs Sheet fact as a question.** If the 5500 shows self-funded, don't ask "are you self-funded?" — ask about the stop-loss layer or the ASO relationship instead.
3. **Prefer open-ended questions over yes/no.** Yes/no belongs on a form, not a discovery call.
4. **Sequence for a real conversation.** Business context first, then benefits specifics, then commercials, then decision process. Employee experience anywhere it fits naturally.
5. **Adapt phrasing to seniority of the person the producer will be talking to.** Same question, different words for the CFO vs. the benefits manager.
