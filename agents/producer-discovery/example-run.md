### 3. Top 5 Must-Ask Discovery Questions

1. **Q:** Who's your current broker, how long have you been with them, and what took the +18% renewal from a surprise to a search?
   - Why: Producer's #1 stated question. The gap between the +18% and the decision to look elsewhere reveals what actually broke trust.
   - Listen for: "we've always been with them" (weak incumbent) vs. "they walked us through options and this was the best available" (strong incumbent).

2. **Q:** What has to be true for you to make a broker change this year?
   - Why: Anchors the whole sales process. Without an answer here, everything else is guessing.
   - Listen for: specific criteria (good) vs. "we'll know it when we see it" (means a longer sale).

3. **Q:** Have you ever looked at self-funded or level-funded arrangements, and if so what stopped you from moving?
   - Why: Fully-insured at 218 EEs is the meaty part of the value conversation. Their prior thinking predicts appetite.
   - Listen for: "our broker never brought it up" (opening) vs. "we looked and decided against it because…" (learn the objection).

4. **Q:** Beyond price, what would make employees notice that their benefits program got better?
   - Why: Moves the conversation off the +18% into value the firm can actually deliver. Also surfaces employee-experience pain the incumbent may have ignored.
   - Listen for: specific complaints (network gaps, service issues, communication) — each maps to a concrete recommendation.

5. **Q:** Who else needs to be at the table for this — David, Anna, anyone on the finance side, board?
   - Why: Warm intros can obscure a real buying committee. Priya may or may not be the sole decision-maker.
   - Listen for: names to add to the map; also whether she's protective or open about the committee.

**Employee experience**

**Compliance & risk**

**Vendor & broker relationships**

**Decision process**
- What does your decision timeline look like — what are the milestones?

- Any collective bargaining coming up? (LinkedIn does not indicate a unionized workforce, but confirming.)
