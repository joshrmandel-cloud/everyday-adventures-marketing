## 5. One Opportunity That Surprised Me

**Grandparents are a sleeper gifting channel — and the market data backs it up.**

Research this week turned up a striking data point: grandparents, aunts, and uncles make up **20% of Nanit's active users** (the smart baby monitor). This isn't a fluke — it's a signal that older family members are actively engaged with apps and tech tools built around their grandchildren's wellbeing. They just need the right framing.

Everyday Adventures' five packs map perfectly onto what grandparents want to give: *experiences, not stuff.* A grandparent who does bedtime or bath time when they babysit would love having a script of tested, fun activities that don't require buying anything physical. The founder story — a dad who built these for his daughters in 2004 — is also a story that *grandparents of those daughters* can feel proud of.

The play: target gift-giving moments (birthdays, holidays, "just because I'm a grandparent" guilt) with messaging like *"The gift that makes bath time the best part of their day."* The Parenting Apps Market is growing at 12.6% CAGR — and grandparents as a gifting segment is largely untapped by competitors. This could be a differentiated marketing lane worth testing with a single Instagram ad creative this quarter.
