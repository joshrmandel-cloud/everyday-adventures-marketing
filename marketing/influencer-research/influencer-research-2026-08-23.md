**Pinterest is quietly becoming the highest-intent marketing channel for Everyday Adventures — and nobody's talking about it.**

Pinterest's 2026 Parenting Trend Report revealed extraordinary growth in exactly the searches that map to Everyday Adventures packs:

- "Screen free activities" — **+200% YoY**
- "Movement activities for toddlers" — **+145%**
- "Indoor fun activities for kids" — **+130%**
- "Family traditions ideas" — **+200%**
- "1970s childhood toys" — **+200%** (nostalgia trend)
- "Vintage kids bedroom" — **+100%**

Parents are actively searching Pinterest for offline, intentional, screen-free things to do with their toddlers. They're not passively scrolling — they're in high purchase and download intent mode.

Unlike Instagram or TikTok where you're competing for attention, Pinterest is a search engine where people come with a specific need. A well-crafted set of Pinterest boards — "Toddler Bath Time Ideas," "Rainy Day Activities for 2-Year-Olds," "Bedtime Routines That Actually Work" — could drive sustainable organic traffic to the Everyday Adventures App Store page for months.

The vintage/nostalgia angle is a bonus: the fact that Josh built these activities in 2004 fits the "timeless childhood" aesthetic that's currently trending across the platform. Pin the founder story alongside the activities.

**Recommended action:** Create a Pinterest business account for Everyday Adventures and publish 5 boards (one per pack) with a combination of activity pins, screenshots, and tips this week. Cost: $0. Potential return: consistent, compound organic reach.
