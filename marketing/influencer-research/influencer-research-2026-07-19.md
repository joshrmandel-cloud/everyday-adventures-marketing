## 1. Influencers & Creators to Check Out

### 1. Big Little Feelings — @biglittlefeelings
**Platform:** Instagram + TikTok + Podcast
**Followers:** ~4.2M on Instagram

**Why relevant:** Hosts Kristin Gallant and Deena Margolin are the dominant voice in toddler emotional development. Their content focuses on tantrums, big emotions, and daily routines — the exact territory Everyday Adventures lives in (bedtime, bath time, mornings). Their Fisher-Price partnership history signals they work with children's product brands.

**Suggested approach:** Follow and engage authentically with comments for 2–3 weeks, then DM with a personal pitch — specifically the bath/bedtime angle. They also host the *After Bedtime* podcast (see Section 3). A brand partnership inquiry can go through biglittlefeelings.com.

### 2. Busy Toddler — @busytoddler
**Platform:** Instagram + blog (busytoddler.com)
**Followers:** ~2.4M on Instagram

**Why relevant:** Susie Allison holds an M.Ed. in Early Childhood Education and taught kindergarten for 8 years. She is the single most credible voice specifically for toddler activities (ages 1–4), which maps directly to Everyday Adventures' content. She has appeared on Good Morning America and published a book and preschool curriculum. Her audience is exactly Josh's target.

The "developed by a dad who tested everything on his own kids" angle aligns with her research-backed approach.

## 5. One Opportunity That Surprised Me

### Pediatric OT and Speech Therapist Influencers — Overlooked but Highest-Trust

A cluster of pediatric occupational therapists has built substantial followings on TikTok and Instagram by sharing sensory and developmental activity tips. These professionals are highly trusted by parents of toddlers — especially for bath time, which has a significant sensory dimension. When a pediatric OT recommends an activity app, parents act immediately.

Key accounts worth approaching:
- **@thesimpleot** — 120K+ TikTok followers, Licensed Pediatric OT
- **@ot_mama_of_twins** — Pediatric OT + mom, Instagram
- **@therapyforkidz** — Pediatric OT specializing in sensory activities

None of these are influencers-for-hire in the traditional sense, which makes their endorsements far more credible than paid placements. The Bath and Rainy Days packs have strong overlap with sensory activities pediatric OTs already recommend. A genuine outreach email — "I built this set of activities for my daughters; would love your thoughts" — is more likely to work than a formal sponsorship pitch.

**Bonus surprise: The "Scholastic Passed on It" angle is a PR story in itself.**
A dad who developed toddler activities in 2004, pitched Scholastic (who passed), and finally launched an app 22 years later is a human-interest hook that local news, parenting newsletters, and founder-story podcasts will pick up. Josh should consider pitching not just parenting podcasts but also "founder journey" shows and local/national media with the Scholastic rejection as the narrative spine. The Founder Hour (thefounderhour.com/submit) explicitly accepts guest submissions and covers exactly this kind of backstory.
